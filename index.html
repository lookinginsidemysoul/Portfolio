About

Analyst by trade, builder by instinct.

Eight-plus years spent making messy datasets tell the truth clearly enough for the C-suite to act on it — and lately, teaching that same instinct to a set of AI agents so the answer arrives before anyone has to ask.

Hi, I'm Pallab.

I started out doing the part of analytics nobody enjoys: chasing down mismatched numbers across spreadsheets, rebuilding the same report every Monday, waiting on someone else to run a query so I could answer a simple question. Eight-plus years and a few hundred dashboards later, I lead BI reporting, data modeling, and delivery for enterprise clients at Infosys BPM — Power BI, Tableau, and SQL Server are still my daily tools.

The work I'm proudest of, though, is the one that got rid of the waiting. I designed and shipped a multi-agent SQL assistant that lets business users just ask for what they need in plain language — no query-writing, no ticket in a queue — and it plans and runs the SQL behind the scenes. It's currently doing the work of 3.2 full-time analysts, and it's what pulled me firmly into building AI systems rather than just reporting on them.
