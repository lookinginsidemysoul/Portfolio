THE THREAD

There is one story underneath all the job titles.

My background looks broad on paper. Up close, it is remarkably consistent: I keep moving closer to the point where data becomes a useful decision — and where other people can use the system without needing me in the room.

I started by teaching people how technology works. I then spent years learning how messy real-world data behaves: quality checks, duplicates, compliance, process controls and operational exceptions.

That foundation led naturally into BI — modelling data, automating reporting, building dashboards and translating requirements into something stakeholders could act on. From there, my role expanded into product support, Agile delivery, stakeholder workshops, team leadership and executive decision support.

Today, the same pattern is showing up in AI. My multi-agent SQL assistant is not an AI demo for its own sake; it is an attempt to remove a recurring bottleneck between a business question and the data needed to answer it.

“I am most useful where the problem is ambiguous, the data is imperfect, and someone still needs to turn it into a decision.”

HOW I WORK

My leadership style is built around the same thread.

I do not see technical delivery, people leadership and transformation as separate disciplines. They reinforce one another when the operating model is clear.

01 · CLARIFY

Start with the decision.

What does someone need to decide, what evidence would change the decision, and what is genuinely worth measuring?

02 · STRUCTURE

Make the data dependable.

Shape the model, KPI definitions, controls and delivery approach before adding visual polish or AI.

03 · AUTOMATE

Remove repeatable friction.

Use automation and AI where they release capacity or improve access — with validation and human judgement still in the loop.

04 · ENABLE

Leave the team stronger.

Coach people, document the system, create reusable patterns and make adoption part of delivery rather than an afterthought.
