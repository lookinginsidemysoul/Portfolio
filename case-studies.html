I built and deployed a multi-agent SQL assistant for natural-language enterprise querying, turning a manual reporting bottleneck into a governed conversational workflow.

02 · Enterprise Data + Controls

Invoicing & Reconciliation Analytics

Enterprise invoicing work taught a less glamorous but more important lesson: analytics only becomes decision-grade when the underlying controls are dependable. I worked across invoicing analytics, reporting automation and reconciliation, including large enterprise billing contexts where client-sensitive details must remain generalized.

CONTROL

Accuracy, reconciliation and traceability are treated as part of the product—not an afterthought.

The operating challenge

  • Multiple sources and reporting views can create reconciliation friction.
  • Exceptions need to be surfaced, not hidden inside aggregate totals.
  • Automation must preserve control points and auditability.
  • Stakeholders need a common view of what is complete, pending or disputed.

Manager-level behaviour

  • Separate data ingestion, validation, reconciliation and presentation concerns.
  • Define exception paths before optimising the happy path.
  • Use repeatable controls so reporting does not depend on individual memory.
  • Translate technical checks into stakeholder-facing operational signals.

03 · Commercial Analytics + Decision Support

From Dashboard to Decision

A commercial analytics engagement moved beyond descriptive reporting into recommendations on promotional spend and operations. The important shift was from “what happened?” to “what should leadership do next?” The documented outcome was a 15% quarterly revenue uplift.

The decision chain

  • Frame the commercial question and decision owner.
  • Combine performance signals into an interpretable view.
  • Identify drivers and opportunity areas rather than reporting every metric.

  • Close the loop by measuring the business outcome.

What makes this leadership-grade
